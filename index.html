    How it works

    You choose a heart rate for each block. Every second, this page reads your strap and nudges the trainer's resistance up or down to bring you to that number.

    Heart rate answers a change in effort about a minute late. Expect to sit within a few beats of target once a block settles, and further off for the first minute or two after a big change.

    Riding with Zwift

    1. Open this page on a second device, such as your phone. Keep it on screen for the whole ride.
    2. In Zwift's pairing screen, pair your trainer as Power Source only. Remove it from Resistance, so Zwift does not fight this page for control.
    3. Start a free ride in Zwift.
    4. Here, connect your strap and trainer, then start the workout.

    If a device will not connect

    1. On iPhone or iPad, use the Bluefy browser. Safari cannot reach Bluetooth devices.
    2. Some older trainers and straps accept only one Bluetooth connection. Disconnect them from other apps first.
